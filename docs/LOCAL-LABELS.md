# Regional label policy

Latest user decision supersedes earlier three-label and twelve-label trials: label county seats, major mountain areas, large lakes and important independent regional areas. No individual attractions or exhaustive central-city district labels. Do not force a name onto every illustrated settlement.

Nanchang: 安义、莲塘、进贤、梅岭、军山湖、红谷滩. These are schematic regional anchors. Original SVG shapes remain unchanged.

Shared features use data/jiangxi-shared-labels.json. Largest displayed lake intersection in the accepted map decides the single owner tile. This is a cartographic placement decision, not a real-world area or jurisdiction claim. 鄱阳湖 is reserved for the owner tile and omitted in Nanchang. New shared mountain systems also require an owner before generation.

City overlay text hides at 5x and returns at or below 4.5x. Full-map export restores city overlay text.
